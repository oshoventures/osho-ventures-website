MINERAL BENEFICIATION / 02

RETHINKING THE MATERIALS BEHIND INFRASTRUCTURE

Cement, construction and industrial manufacturing depend on mineral inputs that meet defined chemical, physical and performance requirements. Beneficiation improves a mineral-bearing feedstock by removing unwanted components or concentrating useful minerals, where the material characteristics and process economics support it.

Osho Ventures evaluates the pathway from material characterisation through process selection, product qualification, infrastructure, logistics and market acceptance. The objective is to identify technically and commercially credible routes for each resource, rather than assume a particular grade, recovery rate or application before testing.

The suitability of a mineral feedstock depends on its mineralogy, particle size, impurities, and variability. SELECTING A PROCESS TO SUIT THE MINERAL AND TARGET PRODUCT.

Depending on the mineral and the intended product, candidate routes may include crushing, screening, washing, sizing, gravity separation, magnetic separation, flotation or other suitable processes. The appropriate route must be established through material-specific testwork and evaluation of technical, environmental and commercial constraints.

  • Candidate beneficiation routes
  • Recovery, yield and material-balance assessment
  • Energy, water and reagent requirements, as relevant
  • Residue, tailings and by-product management

CONNECTING UPGRADED MINERALS WITH VIABLE INDUSTRIAL APPLICATIONS.

A technically recoverable mineral is commercially useful when the resulting product meets the industrial application and economics. Product qualification is considered on the basis of mineralogy, extremal features, properties, consistency, recoverability, available technologies for processing, handling, logistics and market acceptance.
